Welcome to Jain College

This smart campus navigator helps students, visitors, and faculty understand the structure of the college campus in a simple and interactive way. Instead of getting lost or asking ten people where the B.Sc block is, this system gives you a clear campus view and building details.

The campus starts from the Entrance Gate. As you enter, the Sports Complex lies on the right side. From the main route, the path further divides toward the Administration Block and the B.Sc Block. Beyond that, the academic blocks continue in sequence.

What this website does:
• Gives a college introduction
• Explains what each building contains
• Lets you search a destination and get directions
• Shows an interactive campus map with hover and click actions

Campus Flow

Starting Point: Entrance Gate

Right side after entry: Sports Complex

Main branching route: Administration Block / B.Sc Block

Academic sequence after B.Sc:

Mathematics Block → Biology Block → BBA Block → BCom Block → Arts Block → Masters Block → PhD Block
